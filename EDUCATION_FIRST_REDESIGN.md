# Education-First Redesign

## Product position

The public website now positions My Academic Tutor as a tutoring and education service rather than a tutor directory or marketplace.

The student-facing journey is:

Student has an academic problem → learns from MAT content → explores a subject or tutoring service → shares learning requirements → MAT arranges suitable one-to-one support → tutoring begins.

Tutor matching and assignment remain internal CRM operations.

## Main changes

- Homepage hero changed to “Personalised tutoring for deeper understanding.”
- Main CTA changed from “Find a tutor” to “Get personalised tutoring”.
- Navigation now prioritises Subjects, Academic Levels, Tutoring, Learn and AI Tutor.
- Homepage explains the teaching process: clarify → explain → practise → progress.
- Added a full tutoring-services catalogue with seven service pages.
- Added `/learn` as the Learning Hub.
- Existing `/resources` URLs permanently redirect into `/learn`.
- Subject pages now combine educational context, tutoring approach, learning resources and lead capture.
- Academic-level pages focus on how teaching changes by level.
- Lead form language focuses on learning requirements rather than tutor matching.
- Student acknowledgement copy now says MAT will arrange suitable one-to-one support.
- Internal admin statuses and tutor assignment are unchanged.

## New tutoring routes

- `/tutoring/one-to-one-tutoring`
- `/tutoring/exam-preparation`
- `/tutoring/university-tutoring`
- `/tutoring/masters-support`
- `/tutoring/statistics-data-analysis`
- `/tutoring/dissertation-research`
- `/tutoring/coding-software`

## Learning Hub

The Learning Hub contains educational articles covering statistics, mathematics, data science, bioinformatics, research methods and study support. Each article teaches first and only then offers personalised tutoring as the next step.
